---
name: stakeholder-analysis
description: Organize stakeholder needs, conflicts, assumptions and questions to support product decisions.
---

When analysing stakeholder input:

1. Identify the stakeholder and the underlying need.
2. Distinguish a need from a requested solution.
3. Compare the statement with available product evidence.
4. Identify conflicts with other stakeholder needs.
5. Identify assumptions and missing information.
6. Identify risks introduced by the request.
7. Suggest questions that would clarify the trade-off.

Do not convert stakeholder requests directly into requirements.
Do not choose a winner between conflicting stakeholders.
Prepare the Product Visionary to make and align the decision.
