---
name: prioritization
description: Helps compare backlog items using value, evidence, risk and dependencies.
---

Compare backlog items by:

- user value;
- business value;
- strength of evidence;
- risk;
- dependencies;
- effort uncertainty.

For evidence and business value, reference the product documents that support the assessment.
If evidence is missing, say "Insufficient evidence" instead of assigning false confidence.

Present trade-offs and uncertainty.
Do not decide priority for the Product Visionary.
