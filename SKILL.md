---
name: story-refinement
description: Evaluate and refine product user stories for clarity, value and readiness.
---

When analysing a story:

1. Identify the user or stakeholder.
2. Identify the user's problem or need.
3. Identify expected user and business value.
4. Check whether acceptance criteria are understandable and testable.
5. Identify evidence supporting the item.
6. Identify dependencies, risks, assumptions and unanswered questions.
7. Check the item against backlog/definition-of-ready.md.
8. Explain only the readiness criteria that are satisfied, missing, or require clarification.

Never declare a story ready if relevant information is missing.
Do not invent missing facts to make the story appear ready.
